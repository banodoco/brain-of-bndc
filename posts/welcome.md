## Want to become a Speaker? Share a short intro + something you made

Banodoco is a space focused on creating and sharing with open-source AI.

Anyone is welcome to browse and lurk, but for the sake of conversation quality + spam avoidance we require people to share two things to speak:

• **A short intro.** Who are you? What are you interested in? Which models, tools, or workflows are you working with? Tell us something specific — "I'm into AI art" isn't an intro. A link might also help!

• **Something you've made.** A silly generation, a GitHub repo, a workflow, a piece of art, or a rough experiment all count. It doesn't need to be polished or impressive — just something that shows that you're (probably) human and actually care about the purpose of this space.

Then, if you like, tell us **what you want to contribute** — what you're learning, building, or hoping to explore next.

Head over to <#1138861011206688829> and share your intro. **A human will review your intro as soon as possible after you share both** — you don't need to do anything else once it's up. Need help with something in the meantime? Ask in <#1163250319107555388>.
