## Art Compute Micro-Grants

We offer micro-grants of **10-50 GPU hours** (H100, H200, or B200) to support open-source AI art projects. Grants are paid in **SOL** to your Solana wallet — no paperwork, no waiting.

### How it works

**1.** Create a post in this channel describing your project
**2.** Our bot reviews your application using AI and responds within minutes
**3.** If approved, you'll see the grant details (GPU type, hours, cost) and be asked for your Solana wallet address
**4.** Reply with your wallet and the SOL is sent automatically — you'll get a transaction link to verify

If more information is needed, the bot will tell you what's missing — just reply in the same thread and it will re-review.

### Progress updates and delivery evidence

If you receive a grant, post progress and final results in the **same application thread**. Include concrete results, links to relevant commits, releases, demos, or papers, and a short note about what the grant helped you complete. The bot acknowledges substantive progress updates in that thread. Keeping updates together lets future grant reviews consider your progress and delivery evidence alongside your application history. Linked Discord messages are fetched only from public channels that you and the bot can read; GitHub and YouTube links are kept as references and are not treated as verified results by themselves.

Have questions? Ask in the [Questions & Discussion](https://discord.com/channels/1076117621407223829/1479211913652207706) thread.

### Example projects

- **Train new control dimensions for LTX with IC-LoRAs** — you can train a whole new control dimension onto LTX in as little as eight hours
- **Train edit LoRAs for Flux, Qwen Edit, etc.** — add new modalities and control types that help models accomplish tasks they can't do out of the box

### What to include in your application

- **Project description** — what are you building?
- **What the compute is for** — training, fine-tuning, inference, etc.
- **Links to prior work** — GitHub repos, demos, papers, or anything that shows your capability
- **If you already have an active grant** — include its status, scope, and progress so we can assess whether this application is distinct and fits alongside your current commitments
- You can optionally request a specific GPU type or number of hours

### Approval criteria

- Project must be **open-source** (or commit to open-sourcing results)
- **Reasonable scope** — 10-50 GPU hours should meaningfully advance the project
- **Merit and reputation** — public contributions, previous work, and clearly articulated training goals. If you're new, start small.
- **Community benefit** — the project serves the broader AI/ML community

### Grant limits

- Maximum grant: **50 hours of H100** (~$137 USD paid in SOL)
- One active grant is the default limit. Applicants with a strong, verifiable reputation and reliable delivery may be considered for multiple grants at once when each has a distinct scope and combined commitments are manageable.
- Applicants with previous grants should demonstrate concrete progress or results. If an active grant's status, scope, or progress is unclear, provide those details in the application; concurrent requests are not approved on assertion alone.

### GPU rates (approximate)

- **H100 80GB**: $2.50/hr
- **H200**: $3.50/hr
- **B200**: $5.00/hr

A 10% fee buffer is added to cover platform costs.

### Using your grant

You can pay for GPU compute directly with crypto on [RunPod](https://docs.runpod.io/references/billing-information#payment-methods), or convert the SOL to your local currency and use any provider you prefer.
